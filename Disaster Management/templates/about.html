INTRODUCTION

About the Website

Disasters such as floods, cyclones, earthquakes, fires and landslides can cause serious loss of life, property and infrastructure.

During a disaster, timely communication and proper coordination are essential. Delays in receiving information or deploying emergency resources can increase the impact of a disaster.

The Disaster Management System provides a centralized digital platform that connects citizens, administrators and emergency response teams.

Through this platform, users can report incidents, receive emergency alerts, find shelters and access information about response teams and relief resources.
